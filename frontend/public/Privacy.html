Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

Pi Arcade is an independent community app developed by Appnexo PuntoApp. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Pi Network or Minepi, Inc.

2. Information we collect

  • Pi Network user ID and username (provided by Pi SDK authentication)
  • Game activity: spins, results, payouts
  • Balance and withdrawal history
  • Payment records for spins paid with Pi (amounts and Pi payment IDs)

3. How we use your information

  • To run the slot machine game and track your balance
  • To process withdrawals to your Pi Wallet
  • To prevent fraud and duplicate transactions
  • To improve the app experience

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Payment processing is handled entirely by Pi Network's SDK and infrastructure.

5. Data retention

Game records are retained for up to 90 days. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at dpsservicio@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security practices. Your Pi credentials are never stored by our app — authentication is managed exclusively by Pi Network's SDK.

7. Contact

For privacy questions: dpsservicio@gmail.com
